Who We Are

A community, not just a society.

LWiSE is a student-led society at the Lahore University of Management Sciences dedicated to empowering women in science, technology, engineering, mathematics, research, and academia.

We believe the problem is never a lack of talent. It is a lack of access — to opportunities, mentors, research, resources, community, and representation. LWiSE exists to build those bridges.

Featured Initiative — 2026

Research has no gender.

The All-Women National Research Article Competition 2026 was organized by LWiSE in collaboration with SBASSE, LUMS and The Particle Magazine — opening pathways for young women across Pakistan to engage with scientific research and academic writing.

Instead of simply announcing a competition, LWiSE provided research-writing training first — helping participants understand scientific structure, argumentation, and communication. It featured an all-female panel of STEM professionals.
