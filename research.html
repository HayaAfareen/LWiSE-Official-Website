Research & Publications

Research has no gender.

LWiSE believes research should not feel inaccessible. We create pathways — competitions, training, publications, and resources — to bring women into research.

Why it matters

Research feels inaccessible to many students — especially those in their first year or from high school. The competition was not just about finding the best writer. It was about creating a pathway.

LWiSE did not simply announce a competition. It provided hands-on research-writing training to participants first — helping them understand scientific structure, argumentation, and communication before writing a single word.

Participants included high-school students and first-year undergraduates from across Pakistan — well beyond the LUMS campus.

The Particle Magazine

The Particle is the science magazine associated with the Syed Babar Ali School of Science and Engineering (SBASSE). LWiSE's collaboration with The Particle gives members a platform to write about science, build scientific communication skills, and get published.
