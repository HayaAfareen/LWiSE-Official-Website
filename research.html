Research has no gender.

LWiSE creates pathways — competitions, training, publications, and resources — to bring women into research.

Why it matters

Research feels inaccessible to many students — especially those in their first year or from high school. The competition was not just about finding the best writer. It was about creating a pathway.

LWiSE provided hands-on research-writing training before the competition — helping participants understand scientific structure, argumentation, and communication. Participants included high-school students and first-year undergraduates from across Pakistan.

The competition included a cash prize and the winning article was offered publication in The Particle Magazine — a significant first publishing opportunity for young researchers.
